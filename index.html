Hi, I am Xiaolin Liu.

I am a Ph.D. student majoring in Intelligence Science and Technology at the University of Science and Technology of China. I am jointly advised by Prof. Xue Yang and Prof. Shaofeng Zhang. I am also jointly trained at Beijing Zhongguancun Academy.

Before that, I received my M.S. degree in Control Engineering from Shandong University in 2026, and my B.S. degree in Automation from Qingdao University in 2023.

My research interests focus on multimodal large language models, time-series foundation models, agents, and multimodal evaluation benchmarks.
